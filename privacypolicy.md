Privacy Notice:
Discord YouTube is a completely free service and does not collect any personal data from its users.

However, please be aware that YouTube and your web browser (e.g., Chrome/Electron) may collect data according to their own privacy policies.

To protect your privacy, Discord YouTube takes several measures to minimize data exposure and obfuscate your identity as much as possible while providing Rich Presence functionality.

For full transparency, we encourage users to review YouTube's and their browser's privacy policies, and if privacy is a major concern, consider using additional tools such as VPNs alongside Discord YouTube.
