Discord YouTube is a free, open-source Electron app that displays your currently-playing YouTube video as a Discord Rich Presence activity.

By using this software you agree that:
- The app is provided as-is, with no warranty.
- The app reads video metadata (title, channel, playback position, URL) from your YouTube session and sends it to Discord via the official Discord RPC API.
